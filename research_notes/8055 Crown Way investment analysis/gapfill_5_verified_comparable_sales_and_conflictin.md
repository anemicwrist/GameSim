### Inferences
- As-is value to an investor: the deed record puts the investor-purchase tier for 1960s-70s ranches of 2,000-2,850 sf at $111K-$165K ($56-$74/sf) in Feb-May 2026, with the nearest physical analog (7990 Devonshire Dr, 1967, 2,040 sf plus basement, map grid 13-211C beside the subject's 13-211D, county quality F) at $150,000 on 2026-05-13 after a $130,000 deed five weeks earlier (a wholesaler/assignment pattern: $20,000 spread). The subject is better than those homes (county quality A, 3 full baths, roof/windows/panel already replaced, detached garage), so an investor as-is value of roughly $160K-$185K is supportable; the earlier notes' $185K-$225K as-is range is an owner-occupant range, not an investor range.
- ARV: 1692 Pheasant Dr is the paired comp the earlier grid lacked: same decade, same size (2,144 vs 2,117 sf), same bed/bath count, brick-4-sides ranch on a crawl space, fully renovated, sold to an owner-occupant at $285,000 ($133/sf) in March 2026. The subject's extras (detached 2-car garage with workshop, 0.36 ac vs 0.34, 2 fireplaces) versus Pheasant's "2 master bedrooms" and newer furnace/windows roughly offset. With Saddlewood ($325K, 2,210 sf, June 2026) and Deerfield ($316K, 2,468 sf, Aug 2026) above it and Woodknoll ($255K net, 1,764 sf) below, an ARV of $285K-$295K (mid $290K) is better supported than the earlier $275K-$305K band, and the earlier "$280K ceiling" claim is too low: three 1970s ranches over 2,200 sf closed at $310K-$325K in Jun-Aug 2026 (Saddlewood, Deerfield, Choctaw), though their condition is unverified.
- Spread evidence: the Sandpiper Rd pair ($149,500 vs $279,000, one week apart, same 1968 vintage and size) and the Woodknoll flip ($180,000 to $265,000 in 5.3 months with only "updated" cosmetics) both show an as-is-to-finished spread of $85K-$130K in 30238 in 2026, which is consistent with buying the subject at $160K-$185K and reselling near $290K after a $45K-$60K cosmetic renovation.
- List-to-sale behavior: completed sales ran 96.6% (Pheasant), 97.1% (Woodknoll resale, if the $272,900 figure on RE/MAX was its list price; unverified), 88.7% of original list (9542 Canvasback) and 75% of original list (Woodknoll first sale, after 7 months). Concessions of $6,500-$10,000 appear on four of the five sales where the field was visible. A 5-10% below-list offer with 3-5% in concessions on a freshly listed estate property is within the observed range; a 25% below-list price was achieved only after a listing aged 7 months.
- Neighborhood-specific signal: the subject's assessor neighborhood has produced one arm's-length 1968-ranch sale since 2023 ($99/sf in mid-2023, before the 2024-26 decline) and two $0 transfers in 2026, one of them a 2,204 sf 5/3 1968 ranch (8088 Dauphin Dr) that could become a competing estate listing.
