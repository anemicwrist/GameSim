## Key Question 4: Contractor-grade make-ready estimate

### Takeaway

### Cited Findings
- Aluminum branch-wiring remediation: COPALUM crimping $50-$75 per connection point, $3,000-$6,000 for an average home; full rewire $8,000-$20,000 depending on size and accessibility (Maryland electrician's service page, undated) — [A.J.
