## 1. Season, rules, and data sources

**Season and rules**
- **Season M-6** runs 2026-09-09 02:00 UTC → 2026-10-07 (28 days) and uses the Regulation M-C roster — (Game8 "Ranked Battles Season M-6", https://game8.co/games/Pokemon-Champions/archives/620414, updated 2026-09-17, accessed 2026-09-28, T3).
  - The official in-game notice gives the end as **2026-10-07 01:59 UTC** — (Official in-game news "Ranked Battles Season M-6 Underway!", https://news.pokemon-home.com/en/page/822.html, accessed 2026-09-28, T1). Game8's 05:59 UTC is wrong.
- **Regulation M-C** runs "Tuesday, September 8, 2026, 7:00 p.m. PDT to Tuesday, December 1, 2026, 5:59 p.m. PST" (that is, 2026-09-09 02:00 UTC → 2026-12-02 01:59 UTC) — (Pokémon.com "Get Ready for Regulation Set M-C in Pokémon Champions", https://www.pokemon.com/us/news/get-ready-for-regulation-set-m-c-in-pokemon-champions, published 2026-09-02, accessed 2026-09-28, T1).
  - More M-C ranked seasons after M-6 are likely (Game8 expects three) — (S7, T3; OPINION of Game8).
- **Legality:** all Pokémon, regional forms and Megas from the M-B roster, plus the M-C additions. There are no restricted Pokémon — (S7, T3).
- **Clauses:**
  - Species Clause, Item Clause, and one Mega Evolution per battle — (S7, T3).
  - Level 50, pick 3 at Team Preview, Species Clause and Item Clause appeared in **299/299** M-C replays checked. Every previewed Pokémon showed `L50`. — (S4, T2).
  - Teams can register more than one Mega Stone; only one Mega Evolution is allowed per battle — (Smogon BSS Discussion Thread, posts #8 and #10, Jul 2026, S18, T2/T3).
- **Stat system:** Level 50. Stat Points (SP) cap at 32 per stat and total 66.
  - Formulas: HP = Base + SP + 75. Other stats = ⌊(Base + SP + 20) × nature⌋.
  - Checked against PokéChamp DB's public stat code (S1) and against every value in Smogon's Reg M-C speed-tier list (S13). In-game spread data always sums to 66 with a cap of 32 (S1, S10). Details are in `threats.md` §0.

## 2. What changed from M-B (Season M-5) to M-C (Season M-6)

1. **Six new Megas and 24 newly available Pokémon.**
   - The new Megas are Mega Salamence, Mega Golisopod, Mega Baxcalibur and the Z-Megas Mega Absol Z, Mega Garchomp Z and Mega Lucario Z. Rillaboom is among the 24 newly available Pokémon — (Pokémon.com M-C article, https://www.pokemon.com/us/news/get-ready-for-regulation-set-m-c-in-pokemon-champions, 2026-09-02, T1).
   - Game8 counts "23 new Pokémon … not counting alternate forms", including Salamence, Baxcalibur, Golisopod, Rillaboom, Cinderace, Inteleon, Pawmot, Indeedee, Sirfetch'd and Grapploct. This arrived with update 1.2.0 — (Game8 "List of New Pokemon in Regulation M-C", https://game8.co/games/Pokemon-Champions/archives/605517, updated 2026-09-09, T3). The count differs from the official one only because of form counting.
   - **Data:** the new Megas already account for **50% of all Mega Evolutions** in the Showdown sample (178 of 356). Human sides Mega Evolved in 77% of games — (S4, T2).
2. **18 new items**: Rocky Helmet, Air Balloon, Terrain Extender, Red Card, Eject Button, Normal Gem, the Electric/Grassy/Misty/Psychic Seeds, Binding Band, Leek, and 6 Mega Stones — (Game8 "List of New Items", https://game8.co/games/Pokemon-Champions/archives/605519, updated 2026-09-09, T3). They were adopted immediately:
   - **Air Balloon** is on 72.9% of Gholdengo.
   - **Rocky Helmet** is on 62.8% of Corviknight and 54.2% of Gyarados. In M-5, neither Pokémon had Rocky Helmet among its top-5 items.
   - **Psychic Seed / Normal Gem** is on 44.8% / 31.8% of Sneasler.
   - **Terrain Extender** is on 60.8% of Indeedee — (PokéChamp DB in-game M-6 and M-5 Singles data, https://pokechamdb.com/en, snapshots 2026-09-27 and 2026-09-06, accessed 2026-09-28, T2).
3. **Mega Salamence and Garchomp now share the top spot.**
   - The in-game snapshot has **Salamence #1 and Garchomp #2** (Garchomp was #1 all of M-5) — (S1/S2, T2).
   - HazelGrayble's in-game tracker had **Garchomp back at #1 and Salamence #2** by his 9/27 update — (Smogon "Champions Usage Ranking Changelog", https://www.smogon.com/forums/threads/champions-usage-ranking-changelog.3788853/, 2026-09-27, T2 data / T3 commentary).
   - Showdown sample: Salamence 33.8% vs Garchomp 31.0% of teams (S4).
   - **Data:** the two trade places day to day.
4. **The top speed tier rose.** Mega Garchomp Z, Mega Lucario Z and Mega Absol Z are all base 151 Speed, which is **223 Speed** with maximum Speed investment and a Speed-raising nature. The fastest M-B Megas were Mega Greninja (base 142, 213), Mega Lopunny (135, 205) and Mega Delphox (134, 204) — (Smogon "Champions Speed Tiers [Reg M-C]", https://www.smogon.com/forums/threads/champions-speed-tiers-reg-m-c.3780444/, edited 2026-09-12, T2; base stats from Showdown pokedex.json, S15, T2).
   - Mega Delphox fell from **8 → 40** in-game (S1/S2).
   - Top players say: "Delphox benefitted greatly from sitting at the top of the speed tiers. Now that Lucario outspeeds it and First Impression is everywhere, Delphox isn't as hard of a slam-dunk" — (HazelGrayble, S11, 2026-09-27, T3).
5. **Terrain teams arrived** with Indeedee (Psychic Surge) and Rillaboom (Grassy Surge), plus the new Seeds and Terrain Extender.
   - Indeedee is **#21**, new this regulation.
   - Sneasler rose **48 → 15** (Psychic Seed plus Unburden).
   - Mega Blastoise rose **43 → 24** (Terrain Pulse on 74.6% of sets) — (S1/S2, T2).
   - Showdown sample: 9.6% of teams run Indeedee and 13.2% run Rillaboom (S4).
6. **The M-B core shrank.**
   - In-game falls: Meowscarada 3 → 12, Mimikyu 5 → 11, Gyarados 7 → 14, Mega Delphox 8 → 40, Dragonite 9 → 27, Metagross 10 → 22, Scizor 13 → 44.
   - Risers: Lucario 29 → 7 (Mega Z), Gholdengo 24 → 9, Empoleon 74 → 30 — (S1/S2, T2).
   - On Showdown (Aug M-B at the 1500 cutoff vs the M-C sample): Meowscarada 18.7% → 10.0%, Corviknight 15.5% → 7.8%, Gyarados 14.6% → 7.1%. Lucario went 4.3% → 14.9% — (S5 vs S4, T2; the populations differ, so these are indicative only).
7. **Several returning Pokémon changed role.**
   - **Garchomp:** Focus Sash 41.5% → 16.0%. Garchompite Z is now 34.9%. A special set appears (Timid 20.1%; spread 2/0/0/32/0/32 at 23.0%; Earth Power 24.3%; Draco Meteor 30.0%), and Earthquake fell 99.2% → 67.3%.
   - **Gyarados:** Gyaradosite 74.1% → 22.2%. It became an Intimidate + Rocky Helmet + Taunt pivot (Impish 45.2%).
   - **Lucario:** physical Mega (Close Combat, Swords Dance, Meteor Mash) became special Nasty Plot Mega Z (Nasty Plot 76.8%, Dark Pulse 78.6%) — (S1/S2, T2).

## 4. Top archetypes, how each wins, and example teams

Prevalence and win % come from the Showdown sample (S4; % of 281 unique human teams; win % over human sides). Mechanics are taken from Showdown's public data files (moves.json, items.js, abilities.js; S15), which are Tier 2 implementations. Assume Champions behaves the same unless the mechanics file says otherwise.

### 4.1 Mega Salamence + Hippowdon balance ("sand, Yawn, Dragon Dance")
- **Data:**
  - Salamence is on 33.8% of teams. Hippowdon + Salamence appear together on 25 teams, lift 2.0. Gholdengo + Hippowdon: 16 teams, lift 2.3. Aegislash + Hippowdon: 9 teams, lift 2.4.
  - Hippowdon teams won 54.7% of sides (95% CI 43–66, n=64, 30 players) (S4).
  - In-game, Salamence's top partners are Primarina, Hippowdon, Gholdengo, Garchomp, Lucario and Archaludon (S1).
- **How it wins:**
  - Hippowdon sets sand and Stealth Rock (89.5%). Yawn (95.9%; "puts the target to sleep after 1 turn") and Whirlwind (60%) force switches and pile up chip damage.
  - Mega Salamence (Intimidate on entry, then Aerilate Double-Edge: Normal moves become Flying at 1.2×, S15) uses Dragon Dance and Roost to sweep a weakened team.
  - Gholdengo (Good as Gold, Air Balloon) or Primarina covers the special side.
- **Examples:**
  - Tyrant Lucifer (1353): Salamence, Gholdengo, Primarina, Volcarona, Pawmot, Hippowdon, W — https://replay.pokemonshowdown.com/gen9championsbssregmc-2686879618 (S4).
  - Gosaimasu (1455, 3-0 in sample; brought Mega Golisopod): Grimmsnarl, Gholdengo, Golisopod, Hippowdon, Ceruledge, Salamence — https://replay.pokemonshowdown.com/gen9championsbssregmc-2682957566 (S4).
  - Counter-example: DripLegend's Hippowdon / Salamence / Aegislash / Greninja / Golisopod / Hydreigon lost 0-2 to Zerotti in BSSCL II — https://replay.pokemonshowdown.com/smogtours-gen9championsbssregmc-975335 (S4/S16).

### 4.2 Aurora Veil + setup (Alolan Ninetales)
- **Data:**
  - On 13.2% of teams. Human sides **won 61.1% (CI 50–72, n=72, 29 players)**, the best of the large archetypes (S4).
  - The in-game Alolan Ninetales set is Aurora Veil 97.3%, Light Clay 90.3%, Freeze-Dry 72.2%, Encore 70.0% and Blizzard 64.4% (S1).
  - Veil teams Mega Evolve Lucario most often (15; Lucario Megas in the sample are 38 of 42 the Z form), then Baxcalibur (10) (S4).
- **How it wins:**
  - Snow Warning sets snow, which enables Aurora Veil ("damage to allies halved … Snow only"). Light Clay extends Veil to 8 turns (S15).
  - A setup sweeper (Nasty Plot Mega Lucario Z, Swords Dance or Dragon Dance Baxcalibur, Samurott-Hisui, Mega Venusaur) then boosts behind Veil.

### 4.3 Psychic Terrain (Indeedee + Mega Blastoise / Sneasler / Armarouge)
- **Data:**
  - On 9.6% of teams, with very tight cores: Blastoise + Indeedee lift 7.9 (16 teams), Armarouge + Indeedee lift 9.4, Sneasler + Indeedee lift 7.4. Sides won 52.2% (CI 38–66, n=46) (S4).
  - In-game sets:
    - Indeedee: Expanding Force 99.5%, Terrain Extender 60.8%.
    - Mega Blastoise: Shell Smash 91.3%, Terrain Pulse 74.6%.
    - Sneasler: Psychic Seed 44.8% (S1).
- **How it wins:**
  - Psychic Surge sets Psychic Terrain for 8 turns with Terrain Extender. Grounded Pokémon become "priority-safe" in it, and Expanding Force gets 1.5× power and hits all foes (S15).
  - Sneasler's Psychic Seed is consumed, which raises Sp. Def by one stage and triggers Unburden (Speed doubles on item loss) (S15).
  - Mega Blastoise uses Shell Smash, then Terrain Pulse, which "doubles power, type varies" on terrain (S15), without fearing priority revenge-kills.
- **Examples:**
  - **Dua Lipa**: Indeedee, Armarouge, Charizard, Blastoise, Cinderace, Baxcalibur. Beat skype 2-1 in **BSSCL II Week 2** — https://replay.pokemonshowdown.com/smogtours-gen9championsbssregmc-975092, …-975107 (S4/S16).

## 6. Holes in the meta: HANDOFF to Lab

```
TO: Lab
TYPE: HANDOFF
MODE: singles
PRIORITY: before-next-session
CONTEXT: First M-C brief. The Mega speed ceiling is now 223, contact priority dominates, and Veil and terrain teams are winning. The numbers are small-sample.
ASK / PAYLOAD: Test H1–H6 below. Each hole lists the evidence and a confidence level.
NEEDED BY: next session
```

1. **H1: Mega Lucario Z vs a contact-priority meta** (confidence: medium).
   - Aura Guard: "receives 1/2 damage from contact moves" (S15).
   - Most common priority in the top 20 makes contact: Sucker Punch (Golisopod 63.5%, Cinderace 46.2%), Aqua Jet (Basculegion 88.2%, Primarina 67.5%), Shadow Sneak (Mimikyu 95.1%, Aegislash 95.8%), Bullet Punch (Metagross 89.0%), Mach Punch (Pawmot 53.9%), Grassy Glide (Rillaboom 97.1%) (S1; contact flags S15).
   - Common non-contact priority is only Ice Shard (resisted by Steel) and Vacuum Wave (S15).
   - Lucario Z runs special Nasty Plot at 223 Speed (Timid 66.1%) (S1). It won 57% when brought (n=44) (S4).
   - **Ask:** test non-contact revenge killers. Candidates are Scarf Earth Power, Vacuum Wave, Scarf Cinderace Pyro Ball, and Gholdengo, which is immune to Fighting and resists Steel but is hit by Dark Pulse (78.6%).
2. **H2: The speed ceiling at 223** (confidence: high on the numbers, medium on the impact).
   - At Speed-raising nature and max investment, Mega Garchomp Z, Mega Lucario Z and Mega Absol Z all sit at 223.
   - These common Scarf users outspeed them: Meowscarada (288, Jolly), Cinderace (282), Garchomp (253), and Timid Scarf Indeedee (241).
   - **Scarf Basculegion (70.7% of Basculegion) reaches only 214** and loses to Speed-nature Z-Megas (formula in threats §0).
   - **Ask:** build a speed-control / revenge-killing checklist for M-C teams. Include +1 Mega Salamence at 283 (S13).
3. **H3: Is Mega Salamence over-picked?** (confidence: low–medium).
   - It is #1–2 in usage, but won only **40% when brought (CI 29–53, n=60)** (S4).
   - Its faints are often self-inflicted: Double-Edge recoil caused 8 of 48, and Rocky Helmet rose sharply (S1, S4).
   - Top players say: "offence as the best defence against Mega Salamence" (deepseazapdos, S12, 2026-09-11). DerpySuX (BSS Leader), 2026-09-10: "Mence is definitely shaping up to be the best mega but I don't want to call anything too early" (S12).
   - **Ask:** quantify punishers: Rocky Helmet with Flying resists (Steel, Rock, Electric), Ice Shard Baxcalibur, Freeze-Dry Ninetales, and Intimidate cycling.
4. **H4: Veil teams vs scarce screen removal** (confidence: medium).
   - Veil sides won 61% (CI 50–72) (S4).
   - In the in-game move lists (top 10 moves per Pokémon, top 30 Pokémon), the only common screen-breakers are Psychic Fangs (Metagross 76.7%) and Defog (Corviknight 12.5%) (S1).
   - **Ask:** test Taunt leads and screen-breakers (Psychic Fangs / Brick Break users, Defog) against Veil + Lucario Z / Baxcalibur.
5. **H5: Psychic Terrain Shell Smash Blastoise** (confidence: low–medium). Psychic Terrain makes grounded Pokémon "priority-safe" (S15), so the usual priority revenge-kill fails. Blastoise is rising 43 → 24 (S1/S2), and Dua Lipa won a BSSCL II set with it (S4).
   - **Ask:** find non-priority answers. In the sample, Pawmot KO'd Blastoise 3 times, Golisopod and Lucario twice each (small n).
6. **H6: More Ground immunities** (confidence: medium).
   - Earthquake is the #1 move on Garchomp and Hippowdon and #2 on Baxcalibur. Yet 72.9% of Gholdengo hold Air Balloon, Mega Garchomp Z has Levitate, and Salamence, Corviknight and Charizard are Flying (S1, S15).
   - **Ask:** re-check Ground-reliant win conditions and the value of Earth Power / Balloon-popping chip.
