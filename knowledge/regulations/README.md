## How ranked seasons relate to VGC regulation sets

- The game groups several ranked seasons under one regulation: 「いくつかのシーズンごとに『レギュレーション』が設定されます」
  ("a 'regulation' is set every few seasons"), and each regulation fixes which Pokémon can be used — (Official site
  『Pokémon Champions』 バトルについて, https://www.pokemonchampions.jp/ja/battle/, accessed 2026-09-28, Tier 1).
- Each regulation notice announces the set "for Ranked Battles", and each season notice names its regulation (e.g., "Ranked
  Battles Season M-6 will follow Regulation Set M-C") — (Official in-game news #816 and #822, accessed 2026-09-28, Tier 1).
- Play! Pokémon: "Designated series are used for all competitions conducted with Pokémon Champions. Each series is divided
  into regulation sets that may last between one and three months," announced "up to 30 days prior" to the first legal
  competition — (Play! Pokémon VGC Tournament Handbook §2.1.1, https://www.pokemon.com/static-assets/content-assets/cms2/pdf/play-pokemon/rules/play-pokemon-vgc-tournament-handbook-en.pdf,
  rev. 2026-09-01, accessed 2026-09-28, Tier 1).
- "The official VGC rules may vary with each series of Ranked Battles in the in-game ladder. That means that both online &
  real-life events will usually follow such regulations, unless announced otherwise." Example exception: the 2027 Indonesia
  Premier Ball League (Sep 19–20, 2026) used M-B after M-C had started — (Victory Road "Pokémon Champions — Regulations",
  https://victoryroad.pro/champions-regulations/, accessed 2026-09-28, Tier 2).
- **In practice:** ranked Singles, ranked Doubles, and official VGC all use the same **regulation** (legal Pokémon, Megas,
  items), and ranked seasons (about 3–5 weeks) sit inside it. The **format** still differs by mode. Ranked Singles is 3v3
  and closed sheets. Ranked Doubles is 4v4 and closed sheets. Official VGC is 4v4 with Open Team Lists and Bo3. See each file.
