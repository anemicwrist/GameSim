## 2. Battle rules (official events)

All bullets in this section — (Play! Pokémon VGC Tournament Handbook, section as noted,
https://www.pokemon.com/static-assets/content-assets/cms2/pdf/play-pokemon/rules/play-pokemon-vgc-tournament-handbook-en.pdf,
"LAST REVISION: September 1, 2026", accessed 2026-09-28, Tier 1):
- **Doubles, bring 6 pick 4** (§4.1): "each competitor selects four Pokémon from their Battle Team … competitors send out the
  first two Pokémon in their party". Teams have "between four and six Pokémon, depending on the tournament format" (§2.1).
- **Level:** "Pokémon above and below Lv. 50 are permitted but will be auto-leveled to Lv. 50 during battle" (§2.3).
- **Item Clause:** "no two Pokémon may hold the same item" (§2.2). **Species Clause:** "cannot contain more than one Pokémon
  with the same National Pokédex number" (§2.3).
- **Legal sources:** Pokémon obtained in Champions, transferred from Pokémon HOME, or received at an official event or
  distribution (§2.3). Victory Road adds that temporary *and* permanent licences obtained in Champions both count (Victory
  Road regulations page, Tier 2).
- **Abilities and moves:** "any move or Ability available to that Pokémon through normal gameplay", including Hidden Abilities
  and event moves. **Exception: "Pokémon may not have the Battle Bond Ability"** (§2.3).
- **Battle Team must stay unchanged** for the whole event, and all other unlocked Battle Teams must be empty (§2.1).
  Competitors may be asked for Replica Team IDs (§3.2).
- **Mega Evolution:** "You can use Mega Evolution only one time per battle." "Duplicate held items are not allowed." —
  (Official #816, Tier 1).
- **Timers** (§4.3.1): "Team Preview: 90 seconds · Move time limit: 45 seconds · Player time ('Your Time') limit: 7 minutes ·
  Game time: 20 minutes". "Play! Pokémon may decide to make revisions to these limits during the season." The in-game M-C
  notice lists the same values (Official #816, Tier 1).
- **No round timer** (§4.2): "There is no round time … all matches are allowed to run their course of up to three games."
- **Number of games** (§4.2): Swiss may be Bo1 or Bo3, and Bo3 is "strongly recommended" at Regionals and above. "Any top cut
  portion of the competition must be run as best-of-three."
- **Match resolution** (§4.4): if both last Pokémon faint on the same turn, the game software decides the winner by coded rules.
  "Run" is the only acceptable way to forfeit. Intentional draws are not allowed.
- **Unresolved matches** (§4.4.2–4.4.4): late or absent player loses first. Otherwise follow the tie tables (Tie results
  count), then **Sudden Death**: a new game where the first player ahead in remaining Pokémon at the end of a turn wins.
- **Disconnections** (§4.4.1): if the game finishes before reconnection, the result stands. Reconnecting within two turns means
  play continues. Staff may void, replay, go to sudden death, or award the game.
- **What a time-out does in Private Battles** isn't stated in the handbook. Reported: the old rule (more Pokémon remaining,
  then more HP) still applies in Private Battles, while ranked and casual time-outs are draws — (Automaton,
  https://automaton-media.com/en/news/pokemon-champions-new-rules-eliminate-timer-stall-wins-but-possible-exploits-raise-concerns/,
  2026-04-08, Tier 3). UNVERIFIED by testing (the Smogon research thread didn't test Private Battles).

## 3. Open Team List (OTS)

- "The 2026 Pokémon VGC season will utilize an open team list format." All Pokémon information on the list "will be made
  available to the opponent **except for the Pokémon's stats** (Speed, etc.)" (§2.4) — (VGC Tournament Handbook, Tier 1).
  Note: the text still says "2026 season" in the Sep 1, 2026 revision. Victory Road marks **every** 2027-season event on its
  calendar (Regionals, SC, LAIC, PBLs) as OTS — (Victory Road 2027 calendar, https://victoryroad.pro/2027-season-calendar/,
  updated 2026-09-23, Tier 2).
- **What's on the list** (§2.4.1): player name, in-game Trainer name, Player ID, date of birth. For each Pokémon: species and
  form (battle-relevant forms, including gender forms such as Meowstic and Indeedee), Ability, held item, all known moves,
  **all stats** (HP/Atk/Def/SpA/SpD/Spe), and **Stat Alignment** (Champions' name for Nature).
- **What the opponent sees:** species/form, Ability, item, moves and **Stat Alignment**. **Not** the stats. [derived] from
  §2.4 (everything except stats) and §2.4.1 (list contents). Victory Road's list of swapped info matches: "Pokémon species …
  Abilities, Held items, All known moves, Stat Alignment" (Victory Road regulations page, Tier 2).
- **Exchange:** lists are swapped once both players connect and "can be reviewed and referenced at any point throughout the
  match" (§4.3). "Players may not take notes or alter their opponents' list" and must return it at the end (Victory Road, Tier 2).
- **Notes:** players may take notes at any time, including during Team Preview, on paper that is blank at the start of each
  round. No pre-written aids such as type charts (§3.7).
- **Language:** RK9 Labs' Team List Creator auto-translates for judges. At International Championships, lists must also be
  provided in English (§2.4.2).

## 4. Equipment and platform (relevant: the Trainer plays on mobile)

- Devices: Nintendo Switch, Switch Lite, Switch OLED or Switch 2 (§3.2). "The mobile device version of Pokémon Champions is
  supported for Open Play Sessions, VGC Cups and Challenges, and any Celebration Events … **not supported at Regional
  Championships and above**" (§3.2.1) — (VGC Tournament Handbook, Tier 1).
- Connection is internet-only. Players meet in **Private Battles** with a Room ID and must select the correct ruleset. The
  in-game electronic team check validates the team against the room's ruleset (§3.5.1, §5.2.1) — (Handbook, Tier 1).
- Latest game update and system firmware are required. Missing either can mean a penalty up to disqualification (§3.4) — (Handbook, Tier 1).

## 7. Relationship to ranked

M-C is also the in-game ranked regulation ("Announcing Regulation Set M-C for Ranked Battles!" — Official #816, Tier 1), and
ranked Season M-6 follows it (Official #822, Tier 1). Ranked practice under M-C uses the **same legal list, Megas and items**
as official VGC. It differs in format: ranked is Bo1 with closed sheets and time-outs are draws. Official VGC has OTS, Bo3
and tiebreak rules. See `README.md` for the full mapping.
