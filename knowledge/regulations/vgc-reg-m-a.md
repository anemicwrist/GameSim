## Launch-era notes

- Headline mechanic changes at launch (Stat Points, no IVs, status nerfs, move rebalances, Unseen Fist ¼ through Protect,
  PP formula): see `knowledge/mechanics.md`. Most were documented during M-A — (Smogon "Champions Battle Mechanics Research"
  OP, https://www.smogon.com/forums/threads/champions-battle-mechanics-research.3780372/, 2026-04-07 onward, Tier 2).
- Known issues at launch included "When both players' Pokémon Mega Evolve simultaneously under certain conditions, the turn
  order may behave incorrectly" — (Official in-game news "Regarding Current Issues", https://news.pokemon-home.com/en/page/758.html,
  2026-04-09, Tier 1). The Mega Evolution order bug was acknowledged and marked fixed on 2026-04-09 — (Smogon mechanics OP,
  "Acknowledged Bugs", Tier 2).
- v1.0.3 (2026-04-23) fixed, among other things, Unnerve not stopping some Berries and "changes in speed caused by held items"
  not being reflected in ability-activation order — (Nintendo Support update history,
  https://www.nintendo.com/en-gb/Support/Purchases-Subscriptions/Games/How-to-Update-Pokemon-Champions-3079895.html, accessed
  2026-09-28, Tier 1).
