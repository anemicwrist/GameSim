## Changes that arrived with M-B

- v1.1.0 (2026-06-17): "Pokémon and held items added for Regulation Set M-B". v1.1.4 (2026-07-08) and v1.1.5 (2026-08-05):
  bug fixes only — (Nintendo Support update history,
  https://www.nintendo.com/en-gb/Support/Purchases-Subscriptions/Games/How-to-Update-Pokemon-Champions-3079895.html, Tier 1).
- The mobile (iOS/Android) version launched **2026-06-17**, the same day as M-B, with cross-play against Switch — (TPCi press
  release, https://press.pokemon.com/en/releases/Pokemon-Champions-Launches-June-17-on-iOS-and-Android-Devices-The-Poke,
  2026-06-03, Tier 1).
- Champions versions of newly added Pokémon differ from SV. Examples: Make It Rain at 95% accuracy with a −2 SpA drop;
  Rage Fist's power bonus resets on switch-out; Gholdengo and Grimmsnarl without Thunder Wave; Metagross without Heavy Slam
  or Knock Off; Scrafty without Parting Shot; Swampert gains Wave Crash — (Game8 list of changes, Tier 3). Make It Rain and
  Rage Fist are corroborated by (Serebii "Updated Attacks", https://www.serebii.net/pokemonchampions/updatedattacks.shtml,
  accessed 2026-09-28, Tier 2).
- New Mega abilities in M-B include **Fire Mane** (Mega Pyroar: Fire moves ×1.5) and **Eelevate** (Mega Eelektross: immune to Ground
  moves, Spikes, Toxic Spikes and Sticky Web, and +1 to its highest stat after a KO) — (Serebii "New Abilities",
  https://www.serebii.net/pokemonchampions/newabilities.shtml, Tier 2).

## Where it was played

- "Regulation Set M-B is the official format of in-game Ranked Battles from 17 June to 9 September 2026 … and will be used in
  VGC events on the same dates, including the 2026 World Championships. Exceptionally, the 2027 Indonesia Premier Ball League
  (held on 19 and 20 September 2026) is also held with this ruleset." — (Victory Road "Pokémon Champions — Regulations",
  https://victoryroad.pro/champions-regulations/, accessed 2026-09-28, Tier 2)
- **2026 World Championships (VGC):** 2026-08-28 → 08-30, San Francisco (Moscone Center), format code "chmb" — (Liquipedia
  "Pokemon Championships/Worlds/2026/VGC", https://liquipedia.net/pokemon/Pokemon_Championships/Worlds/2026/VGC, API data
  accessed 2026-09-28, Tier 2).
- Smogon usage stats for the Showdown M-B ladders (Singles `gen9championsbssregmb`, Doubles `gen9championsvgc2026regmb`) exist
  through 2026-08 — (Smogon stats, https://www.smogon.com/stats/2026-08/, accessed 2026-09-28, Tier 2). They're useful for
  meta-cycle history, not for the current meta.
