## 1. Dates and schedule

- Season M-6 dates, regulation, and the one-week lock are the same as Singles: "Wednesday, September 9, 2026, at 02:00 UTC to
  Wednesday, October 7, 2026, at 01:59 UTC … The Champion Tier and Rank 1 through Rank 3 of the Master Ball Tier will be
  opened on Wednesday, September 16" — (Official in-game news "Ranked Battles Season M-6 Underway!",
  https://news.pokemon-home.com/en/page/822.html, accessed 2026-09-28, Tier 1).
- Doubles and Singles are separate ladders with separate rewards — (Game8 Ranked Battles Guide,
  https://game8.co/games/Pokemon-Champions/archives/588870, updated 2026-09-09, Tier 3).

## 2. Format

- **Bring 6, pick 4** — (Game8 formats, https://game8.co/games/Pokemon-Champions/archives/588873, updated 2026-04-23, Tier 3).
  Serebii: "Team of 4 to 6 Pokémon from Level 1 to Level 100. All Pokémon then set to Level 50" — (Serebii "Season M-6",
  https://www.serebii.net/pokemonchampions/rankedbattle/seasonm-6.shtml, accessed 2026-09-28, Tier 2). Showdown's Reg M-C
  Doubles ladder uses the same structure (`|teampreview|4`, Species Clause, Item Clause, L50) — (Showdown replay,
  https://replay.pokemonshowdown.com/search.json?format=gen9championsvgc2026regmc, accessed 2026-09-28, Tier 2).
- **Item Clause, one Mega per battle, timers** (Total 20 min, Player 7 min, Turn 45 s, Preview 90 s) — (Official in-game news
  "Regulation Set M-C (Updated on September 9)", https://news.pokemon-home.com/en/page/816.html, accessed 2026-09-28, Tier 1).
- **Species Clause** by National Dex number — (Victory Road "Pokémon Champions — Regulations",
  https://victoryroad.pro/champions-regulations/, accessed 2026-09-28, Tier 2).
- **Time-out:** Total Time running out in ranked is a **draw** with no VP and no rank change. "Your Time" running out is a loss.
  An expired move timer uses the top move — (Game8 timers, https://game8.co/games/Pokemon-Champions/archives/596247,
  updated 2026-04-23, Tier 3. Automaton, 2026-04-08, Tier 3). This is **different from official VGC**, where Bo3 tiebreakers
  and sudden death apply (see `vgc-reg-m-c.md`).
- **Idle rule** (tested in Doubles): selecting no actions for 3 turns loses the battle. Selecting only the left Pokémon's action
  counts as not idle. In one test, 2 idle turns were enough — (Smogon "Champions Battle Mechanics Research" p.7, DaWoblefet stream
  #6, 2026-04-15, https://www.smogon.com/forums/threads/champions-battle-mechanics-research.3780372/page-7, Tier 2).

## 6. What Team Preview shows (ranked Doubles)

Same as Singles (§8 of the Singles file): all 6 species and forms and shiny status are shown. **Held items are UNVERIFIED.**
v1.2.0 "Selection Support" indicators appear on your own selection screen. Ranked uses **closed** sheets. The opponent doesn't
see moves, abilities or spreads (no source says otherwise). Contrast official VGC, which uses **Open Team Lists** with species,
ability, item, moves and Stat Alignment visible, but not stats — (VGC Tournament Handbook §2.4,
https://www.pokemon.com/static-assets/content-assets/cms2/pdf/play-pokemon/rules/play-pokemon-vgc-tournament-handbook-en.pdf,
rev. 2026-09-01, Tier 1).

## 7. Doubles-specific mechanics to know (details and citations in `mechanics.md`)

- **Unseen Fist** (and Mega Excadrill's **Piercing Drill**): contact moves go through Protect for **¼ damage**, not full damage.
- **Fake Out** and **First Impression** can't be selected after the user's first turn out.
- **Protect/Detect have 8 PP** (SV: 16 max). Wish and Strength Sap were cut to 8 PP in v1.2.0.
- **Milk Drink** can target an ally, and heals through Protect.
- **Healer** activates 50% of the time (in-game text; SV: 30%).
- An **Encored** Pokémon uses the Encored move's priority bracket. Prankster and Grassy Glide priority update after Encore (M-C-era test).
- **Eject Button** no longer blocks the switch from U-turn/Volt Switch (report from 2026-09-09).
- **Rage Powder** is still a powder move (Grass types are immune); only the display changed in M-C.
