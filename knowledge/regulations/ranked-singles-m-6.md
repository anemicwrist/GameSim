## 2. Format

- **Bring 6, pick 3**, Single Battles. BSS rules as Smogon states them: "All Pokémon will be set to Level 50 automatically.
  Players pick 3 of their 6 Pokemon at team preview. Species Clause: No two Pokemon in the same team can have the same
  National Pokedex number. Item Clause: No two Pokemon in the same team can hold the same item." — (Smogon "Pokemon Champions
  BSS Discussion Thread" OP, https://www.smogon.com/forums/threads/pokemon-champions-bss-discussion-thread.3785396/, 2026-07-17,
  accessed 2026-09-28, Tier 2). Game8 gives Singles as "Bring 6, pick 3" — (Game8 formats,
  https://game8.co/games/Pokemon-Champions/archives/588873, updated 2026-04-23, Tier 3).
- Team registration: Serebii lists the Singles ruleset as "Team of 3 to 6 Pokémon from Level 1 to Level 100. All Pokémon then
  set to Level 50" — (Serebii "Season M-6", https://www.serebii.net/pokemonchampions/rankedbattle/seasonm-6.shtml, accessed
  2026-09-28, Tier 2). The in-game minimum team size is UNVERIFIED. Register 6 regardless.
- **Item Clause:** "Duplicate held items are not allowed." **Mega:** "Certain Pokémon can Mega Evolve if they're holding a Mega
  Stone. You can use Mega Evolution only one time per battle." — (Official in-game news "Regulation Set M-C (Updated on
  September 9)", https://news.pokemon-home.com/en/page/816.html, accessed 2026-09-28, Tier 1). You can carry several different
  Mega Stones, but only one Pokémon Mega Evolves per battle (follows from the two rules above).
- **Species Clause:** by National Pokédex number, so regional forms and Rotom forms count as the same species — (Victory Road
  "Pokémon Champions — Regulations", https://victoryroad.pro/champions-regulations/, accessed 2026-09-28, Tier 2). Showdown's
  BSS Reg M-C ladder applies "Species Clause" and "Item Clause" — (Showdown replay gen9championsbssregmc-2689013474,
  https://replay.pokemonshowdown.com/gen9championsbssregmc-2689013474.json, accessed 2026-09-28, Tier 2). Game8's M-6 page
  words it ambiguously ("…same Species other than Regional Forms"). Treat regional forms as the **same** species.
- **No other clauses are listed** in the official regulation text (no Sleep, OHKO, Evasion or Baton Pass clause) — (Official
  #816, accessed 2026-09-28, Tier 1). The moves exist in the game: Sheer Cold, Fissure, Horn Drill and Guillotine (8 PP, 30%
  accuracy), Double Team, Minimize, Baton Pass, Shed Tail, Hypnosis, Yawn — (Serebii "Useable Moves",
  https://www.serebii.net/pokemonchampions/moves.shtml, accessed 2026-09-28, Tier 2). Whether the in-game "Check Rules" screen
  lists extra restrictions is UNVERIFIED (Trainer: screenshot it).
- **Timers:** "Total Time: 20 minutes · Player Time: 7 minutes · Turn Time: 45 seconds · Preview Time: 90 seconds" — (Official
  #816, accessed 2026-09-28, Tier 1).
- **What happens at time-out (ranked):**
  - Total Time runs out: the battle is a **draw**, with no VP and no rank change — (Game8 "All Battle Timers and Time Limits",
    https://game8.co/games/Pokemon-Champions/archives/596247, updated 2026-04-23, Tier 3). Automaton adds that ranked and casual
    time-outs are an "unconditional draw" and the old count-and-HP rule applies only in Private Battles — (Automaton,
    https://automaton-media.com/en/news/pokemon-champions-new-rules-eliminate-timer-stall-wins-but-possible-exploits-raise-concerns/,
    2026-04-08, Tier 3). Smogon notes the in-game tip (Gameplay Guides → Basic Battling Know-How → Time Limits) "specifically
    says only Casual or Ranked play" — (Smogon mechanics thread p.2, DaWoblefet, 2026-04-09,
    https://www.smogon.com/forums/threads/champions-battle-mechanics-research.3780372/page-2, Tier 2).
  - "Your Time" runs out: "Running out of this time will result in the battle ending and a loss for the player." Move timer
    runs out: "the Pokemon will use the move on the top of the list" — (Game8 timers, same URL, Tier 3).
  - Idle: not selecting actions for 3 turns from battle start loses the battle ("You have been idle for too long!"), tested in
    a Doubles battle — (Smogon mechanics thread p.7, DaWoblefet stream #6, 2026-04-15,
    https://www.smogon.com/forums/threads/champions-battle-mechanics-research.3780372/page-7, Tier 2).
  - Online Competitions (e.g., the Monthly Challenge Series) are **different**: "If the total time allocated for a battle runs
    out, the battle will end, and its result will be determined by the number of remaining Pokémon and their HP." — (Official
    in-game news "The Monthly Challenge Series September 2026 Is Coming!", https://news.pokemon-home.com/en/page/824.html,
    accessed 2026-09-28, Tier 1).

## 5. Rank tiers and how ranking up works

- Official description: "You'll move up or down through the ranks based on the number of wins and losses you accumulate. If you
  reach the Master Ball Tier, you'll then compete with your fellow Trainers for your final placement, which is based on your
  rating." — (Official #822, Tier 1).
- Tiers: Beginner (a tutorial tier), then Poké Ball, Great Ball, Ultra Ball and Master Ball (each Rank 4 → Rank 1), then
  Champion — (GamesRadar+ "All Pokemon Champions ranks and how to rank up",
  https://www.gamesradar.com/games/pokemon/pokemon-champions-ranked-tiers/, updated 2026-06-17, Tier 3. Game8 ranked guide,
  https://game8.co/games/Pokemon-Champions/archives/588870, Tier 3).
- **Tier floor:** "Once you reach a higher tier, you can no longer go down a tier even if you lose consecutively. However, you
  may still decrease in rank." — (Game8 ranked guide, Tier 3. Vandal, https://vandal.elespanol.com/en/pokemon-champions/pokemon-champions-ranks.html,
  2026-07-08, Tier 3).
- **Win streaks** speed up the climb — (Serebii "Ranked Battle", https://www.serebii.net/pokemonchampions/rankedbattle.shtml,
  accessed 2026-09-28, Tier 2. Vandal, Tier 3). "Each rank itself requires at least three wins, but each loss sets you back" —
  (GamesRadar+, Tier 3). The exact point values are UNVERIFIED. Unaudited guide sites report that a win fills ¼ of the rank
  gauge, a loss drains ¼, and wins beyond 2 in a row fill 2/4. That fits "at least three wins per rank", but treat it as a lead.
- **Season reset:** progress "is reset to Poke Ball Tier, Rank 4 at the start of each" season — (GamesRadar+, Tier 3. Vandal,
  Tier 3).
- **Separate ladders:** "Your rank in the Single Battle format does not carry over to the Double Battle format and vice versa.
  Each format also has their own rewards" — (Game8 ranked guide, Tier 3).
- **VP from battles:** 300 VP per win in every tier. The first time you reach Great, Ultra or Master Ball you get 1,000 VP, a
  title and +5/+5/+10 box storage — (Serebii "Ranked Battle", Tier 2).
- Time-out draws give no VP and no rank change (see §2).

## 10. Scout's read (OPINION, not fact)

- With no Heavy-Duty Boots, Stealth Rock and Spikes chip can't be avoided by item. Hazard setters, hazard control (Rapid Spin,
  Defog, Court Change) and Magic Bounce users (e.g., Mega Clefable) may matter more than in SV BSS.
- Choice Band, Choice Specs and Assault Vest are gone, so raw power comes from Megas, Life Orb, type-boost items and setup.
  Choice Scarf is the only Choice item.
- A time-out is a draw, not a win on count or HP. Stalling out the clock while ahead is **not** a win condition in ranked.
