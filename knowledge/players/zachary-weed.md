## Preferred archetypes
- **DATA:** in the Champions era, the **Mega Froslass + Mega Scovillain hyper offense** (Froslass / Scovillain / Lycanroc-Dusk / Sneasler / Basculegion / Kingambit). He played it at NAIC (52nd) and then at Worlds (3rd).
- **DATA:** before Champions, several finishes on Sneasler + Kingambit (+ Basculegion) cores (Limitless, T2).
- **MY READ:** he stays loyal to an archetype across events, and Sneasler + Kingambit is his constant. His M-C Baltimore team kept both.

## Signature tech (Worlds open team list, Limitless T2)
- **Sneasler:** White Herb Unburden, Adamant; Close Combat / **Gunk Shot** / Fake Out / Protect. Most Sneasler run Dire Claw.
- **Mega Froslass:** Timid; Blizzard / Shadow Ball / **Aurora Veil** / Protect. Showdown's data gives Mega Froslass Snow Warning, so Blizzard can't miss and Aurora Veil works (play.pokemonshowdown.com/data/, T2).
- **Mega Scovillain:** Bold; Overheat / Giga Drain / **Rage Powder** / Protect.
- **Lycanroc-Dusk:** Focus Sash; Rock Slide / Close Combat / **Accelerock** / Protect.
- **Basculegion:** Jolly Life Orb (most were Adamant).
- **Kingambit:** Black Glasses with Swords Dance.
