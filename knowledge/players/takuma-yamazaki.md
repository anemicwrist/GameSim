## Preferred archetypes
- **DATA:** Worlds (M-B): the "Dragonite KBS" team (his name for it). Mega Dragonite as the main Mega, Mega Floette as the second, the Kingambit + Basculegion + Sneasler core, and Scarf Garchomp (note, T3).
- **DATA:** PJCS (M-A): a Mega Slowbro team with Farigiraf and Snorlax (VR, T2).
- **MY READ:** the two teams have little in common, so there is no stable archetype yet. The report stresses finding an edge through team building rather than copying the standard (T3).

## Signature tech (Worlds open team list, T2; reasons from his report, T3)
- **Mega Dragonite:** Multiscale, Modest, max Sp. Atk and Speed; Dragon Pulse / Heat Wave / **Extreme Speed** / Protect. Extreme Speed is for Tailwind teams and for KOing a damaged Whimsicott before it moves.
- **Sneasler:** Focus Sash, **Poison Touch**; Close Combat / Dire Claw / Fake Out / **Feint**. Feint breaks Protect and is +2 priority; Poison Touch adds chip, especially off Fake Out.
- **Kingambit:** Chople Berry, **no Protect**; Sucker Punch / Kowtow Cleave / **Low Kick** / Iron Head. No Speed investment; heavy Sp. Def to survive a Timid Mega Floette's Light of Ruin (15/16).
- **Basculegion:** Life Orb (changed from Mystic Water). Sp. Def-heavy to survive a 212-SpA Mega Gengar Shadow Ball (13/16) and a 205-Atk Kingambit Sucker Punch (9/16).
- **Choice Scarf Garchomp:** covers Mega Aerodactyl, Mega Delphox and Mega Raichu Y. Earthquake over Rock Tomb in the last slot.
- **Mega Floette:** Timid to outspeed Jolly Hisuian Arcanine (156) and survive its Head Smash; Light of Ruin instead of Calm Mind.

**Self-reported only** (his report, T3); not yet measured on film.
- **Default bring:** Dragonite + Sneasler lead, Kingambit + Basculegion in the back.
