## Lessons
- L1 — era: champions | mode: singles-transferable | topic: win-condition planning under a selection guess | basis: player plan for this matchup (not confirmed in game 3).
  When the opponent's two possible key Pokémon (here, two Megas) each call for a different answer, anchor the bring on the win condition that beats the rest of their team (Scarf Garchomp against the non-Mega core). Then treat whichever key Pokémon appears as the thing to remove first. — (Yamazaki report, https://note.com/rapid_mint7926/n/nf439490f62a6, 2026-09-06, Tier 3)
- L2 — era: champions | mode: singles-transferable | topic: trading resources (accepting status) | basis: player plan.
  Take a burn on a Pokémon that isn't your closer (Basculegion) if that is what it costs to remove the Pokémon enabling the opponent's plan (Mega Scovillain), before your closer comes in. — (Yamazaki report, URL above, 2026-09-06, Tier 3). Mechanic note: the burn-on-contact behaviour of Mega Scovillain's Spicy Spray is implied by the report and by press, but is not yet verified against a Tier 1 source for Champions. Record it in knowledge/mechanics.md once confirmed.
- L3 — era: champions | mode: singles-transferable | topic: speed control via Choice Scarf | basis: player's own team description.
  A team without Tailwind or Trick Room relied on one Choice Scarf attacker as its only way to move first against this archetype. When you drop a speed-control move, check which matchups leave you with a single faster Pokémon. — (Yamazaki report, URL above, 2026-09-06, Tier 3)
