## Lessons
- L1 — era: champions | mode: singles-transferable | topic: best-of-3 adaptation / team preview | basis: player plan (not confirmed in this game).
  In game 1 against a known archetype, when the opponent has little information about your team, bring your broadest, "basic" four. From game 2, key your lead to what the opponent actually led: here, Kingambit in front whenever they lead Basculegion. — (Yamazaki report, https://note.com/rapid_mint7926/n/nf439490f62a6, 2026-09-06, Tier 3)
- L2 — era: champions | mode: doubles-only | topic: priority vs Prankster speed control | basis: player plan.
  +2 priority attacks (Extreme Speed, Feint) move before a Prankster support move (+1), so a weakened Whimsicott can be KO'd before it sets Tailwind or uses Encore. Yamazaki chose Extreme Speed on Dragonite and Feint on Sneasler partly for this. — (Yamazaki report, URL above, 2026-09-06, Tier 3). Mechanic check: Prankster's +1 priority on status moves (Tier 1 mechanics source still to be cited in knowledge/mechanics.md).
