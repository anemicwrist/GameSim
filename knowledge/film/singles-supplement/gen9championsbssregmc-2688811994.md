## Key Decisions
1. **Leads: Sash against Sash.** On T1 each lead broke the other's Focus Sash, and Samurott-H also laid Spikes. On T2
   Samurott-H's Aqua Jet decided the 1%-against-1% standoff. My read: Samurott-Hisui's lead kit (Sash, priority, and an
   attack that sets hazards) beats a Sash Alakazam in a straight trade.
2. **T2: Ch3t1x leaves the 1% Alakazam in.** It fainted to Aqua Jet before acting. My read: correct. A 1% Alakazam
   with Spikes on its side had no future, because Spikes would take 1/8 on any re-entry. Letting it faint gave Gyarados
   a free entry, and Gyarados, as a Flying type, ignores Spikes.
3. **T6: Ghostofcolress uses Dragon Dance with the new Mega Charizard X instead of attacking.** It took a +1 Waterfall
   (72%) first, because at +0 it was slower. My read: the right idea. At +1 it outspeeds a +1 Gyarados (base Speed 100
   against 81), and Thunder Punch is 2x on Mega Gyarados (Water/Dark), so after the Dragon Dance Charizard was
   threatening to win the 1-on-1 with Gyarados. An immediate Thunder Punch at +0 risked leaving Gyarados alive to KO
   it on T7.
4. **T7: Ch3t1x switches Mega Gyarados out to Garchomp.** This one is quizzed in
   `knowledge/film/positions/ss-gen9championsbssregmc-2688811994-t7.md`. Garchomp is immune to Thunder Punch, and
   Rough Skin (1/8) plus Rocky Helmet (1/6) do 29% per contact hit, more than Charizard's remaining 28%.

## Turning Points
1. **T1–T2:** Samurott-Hisui won the lead trade (Aqua Jet after both Sashes broke) and got Spikes up.
2. **T4–T5:** Mega Gyarados (+1) KO'd Samurott-Hisui and Ninetales-Alola in two turns. The Ninetales KO was a critical

## Lessons
- **Sash against Sash leads: priority decides the standoff.** After both Sashes break, the side with a priority move
  (Aqua Jet here) wins the 1%-against-1% exchange. `[era: singles-supplement | mode: singles | topic: leads]`
- **A 1% Pokémon with no priority behind your own hazards is a free switch, not a fighter.** Let it faint and bring
  your sweeper in for free. `[era: singles-supplement | mode: singles | topic: sacking]`
- **Count passive damage.** Rough Skin or Iron Barbs (1/8) plus Rocky Helmet (1/6) is about 29% per contact hit. A
  switch-in that punishes contact can win against a low-HP physical attacker without ever moving.
  `[era: singles-supplement | mode: singles | topic: endgame-counting]`
- **Equal boosts keep the base-Speed order.** A +1 Charizard (base 100) outspeeds a +1 Gyarados (base 81). Compare
  boosted speeds before you trade hits. `[era: singles-supplement | mode: singles | topic: speed]`
- **Flying types ignore Spikes.** Gyarados came in on two layers' worth of setup without taking hazard damage, while
  Garchomp paid 1/6. `[era: singles-supplement | mode: singles | topic: hazards]`
