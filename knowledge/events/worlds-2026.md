**PLAYERS: the champion's own report** — (natsumewato, "WCS2026 優勝 カイリューKBS", note.com/rapid_mint7926/n/nf439490f62a6, pub. 2026-09-06, T3). The author is identified as Takuma Yamazaki: the title claims the WCS2026 win and the team matches the one above, and Victory Road's X account tags him as @natsumewato (seen in search results only, T4 for the handle link). The Scout translated and paraphrased:
- **Name and core.** The team is "Dragonite KBS". Kingambit, Basculegion and Sneasler ("KBS") are, in his words, the strongest unit in the format.
- **Mega Dragonite** is the main Mega:
  - Multiscale lets it lead safely into most teams, and it beats the common Charizard + Venusaur.
  - Extreme Speed (+2) handles Tailwind teams and KOs a damaged Whimsicott before it can use a support move.
  - Stat Points: maximum Sp. Atk and Speed.
- **Mega Floette** is the second Mega, brought into Froslass and into Floette/Staraptor teams. It is Timid so it outspeeds Jolly Hisuian Arcanine (156) and survives its Head Smash.
- **Sneasler** uses Fake Out and **Feint** (+2, breaks Protect) to force guesses. He chose Poison Touch for poison chip in a priority-heavy team, and Dire Claw over Poison Jab.
- **Kingambit** runs four attacks with no Protect and a Chople Berry for the mirror. It has no Speed investment (so it can move under Trick Room) and heavy Sp. Def to survive Mega Floette's Light of Ruin.
- **Scarf Garchomp** covers the Pokémon that outspeed Sneasler without speed control: Mega Aerodactyl, Mega Delphox and Mega Raichu Y. Earthquake beat Rock Tomb for the last slot.
- **Standard bring:** Dragonite + Sneasler lead, Kingambit + Basculegion in the back. All four have priority moves, so stacking two priority moves reaches KOs from unexpected ranges.
- **His matchup notes:**

  | Opponent archetype | His rating or result |
  |---|---|
  | "Big 6" (Charizard, Floette, Whimsicott, Garchomp, Kingambit, Basculegion) | The finals matchup; he gives two bring patterns for it |
  | Aerodactyl / Kingambit / Sylveon + Farigiraf | Quarterfinal; "clearly unfavorable" |
  | Froslass / Lycanroc | Semifinal; unfavorable |
  | Rain Perish (Politoed / Swampert / Gengar / Vivillon) | Faced in Day 1 round 1; "quite unfavorable" |
  | Delphox / Blastoise | His one Day 1 loss |

- **His own explanation of the win:** opponents hadn't studied the team. He credits a teammate (named in the report) with sharing and tuning it, and says he had no prior results.

## Standout tech (open team lists, T2)

- **Special Mega Dragonite:** Modest; Dragon Pulse / Heat Wave with Extreme Speed (Yamazaki) or Thunderbolt with Inner Focus (Wakasugi).
- **Feint Sneasler:** on the Poison Touch Sash set (Yamazaki) and the Unburden White Herb set (Forbes, 12th).
- **Kingambit Low Kick + a resist berry** for the mirror: Chople on Yamazaki, St. Denis, Piscitelli; Occa on Onishi.
- **Mega Charizard Y's last slot split:** Ancient Power (Onishi, Piscitelli, Kimura) vs Solar Beam (Sánchez, Greppi, St. Denis).
- **Mega Scovillain Rage Powder + Mega Froslass Aurora Veil + Focus Sash Lycanroc-Dusk Accelerock** (Weed, Leite). Mega Froslass's ability is Snow Warning and Aurora Veil works only in snow — (Showdown data, play.pokemonshowdown.com/data/pokedex.json and moves.json, T2).
- **Toxapex** Baneful Bunker / Infestation / Toxic / Wide Guard behind sun, and **Quiet Sylveon** with Yawn / Quick Attack / Detect (Sánchez).
- **Mega Aerodactyl** Tailwind + Ice Fang, and Farigiraf Thunderbolt Trick Room (Greppi).
- **Rare picks:**
  - Mega Blastoise with Shell Smash + Water Spout (Justin Tang, 14th)
  - Mega Blaziken with Torkoal Eruption and Farigiraf (Filip Woźniak, 15th)
  - Kleavor Stone Axe + Feint with Mega Metagross (Chloe Bourke, 16th)

## What Worlds changed next

- The Worlds broadcast teased Reg M-C — (Pokemon.com M-C article, pub. 2026-09-02, T1).
- The closing ceremony announced Z Mega Evolutions (Mega Lucario Z, Mega Garchomp Z, Mega Absol Z) plus Rillaboom and Baxcalibur for Sep 9, and revealed Mega Heatran — (PocketMonsters.net relay of the official Pokemon.com closing recap, pocketmonsters.net/news/9487, pub. 2026-08-31; the original returned 403 to automated access; T4 relay of T1 text).
- **In M-C, the Worlds core lost its hold.**
  - At Baltimore, Kingambit fell to 25.9% and Basculegion to 17.5%.
  - Mega Garchomp Z, not the Worlds-standard Scarf Garchomp, won Frankfurt and Brisbane.
  - See `knowledge/meta/doubles/brief-2026-09-28.md` §1.
