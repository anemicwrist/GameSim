**MY READ:** the Baltimore Rillaboom + Raichu-Y + Gholdengo core (brief §3A), with three changes:
- Volcarona instead of Hisuian Arcanine, adding Rage Powder redirection and a second Tailwind source.
- Mega Garchomp Z as a second Mega option. Showdown's data lists it as pure Dragon with Levitate and base Speed 151 (play.pokemonshowdown.com/data/pokedex.json, T2).
- Three Fake Out users (Incineroar, Rillaboom, Raichu).

## Trends (DATA: top-8 lists only; full usage not yet published)

- **Incineroar was on 7 of 8 teams**, Rillaboom on 5, Sneasler on 4. Gholdengo and Garchomp were on 3 each. Volcarona, Kommo-o, Sylveon, Mega Charizard Y, Mega Dragonite and Mega Floette were on 2 each. (RK9, T1; Scout tally)
- **Mega Stones across the top 8:**

- **No Mega Salamence in the top 8.** A week earlier it was the most-registered Mega at Baltimore (37.3% of Phase 1 teams, Victory Road Baltimore page, T2).
- **The Worlds sun core carried over.** Odriozola's six species match Antonio Sánchez's Worlds 5th-place team (Limitless Worlds team list, T2).
- **The M-A/M-B Charizard Y + Farigiraf Trick Room base** re-appeared with Mega Garchomp Z and Torkoal (Liu Li, 2nd).

## Standout tech (RK9, T1)

- **Special Mega Garchomp Z**, Modest in both finalists' lists. Rios ran Dragon Pulse / Earth Power / Power Gem; Liu Li ran Draco Meteor / Flamethrower / Power Gem.
- **Mega Meowstic-F:** Competitive (its base ability), Timid, Expanding Force / Thunderbolt / Fake Out / Protect. Paired with Focus Sash Indeedee-M carrying Trick Room and Imprison, and Kommo-o Clangorous Soul (Vlieger). Showdown's data gives Mega Meowstic-F the ability Trace (T2).
- **Kommo-o Clangorous Soul** (Leftovers, Soundproof, Modest) in two top 8s (Vlieger, Liuzzo).
- **Quiver Dance Volcarona** with a Grassy Seed (Visser), alongside Mega Glimmora with Spiky Shield.
- **Salamence-free Mega Dragonite with Tailwind** on two Floette teams (Batchelor, Musicco).
- **Taunt Incineroar** (Musicco).
