## Trends (DATA)

- **Mega Evolutions:**

  | Phase | Top Megas (Victory Road, T2) |

  | Phase 1 | Floette 37.2%, Charizard Y 26.0%, Froslass 14.5%, Tyranitar 9.2%, Scovillain 9.1%, Aerodactyl 8.5%, Delphox 8.5%, Dragonite 8.0% |
  | Phase 2 | Floette 39.7%, Charizard Y 31.4%, Froslass 25.0%, Scovillain 18.6%, Dragonite 9.6%, Aerodactyl 9.6%, Delphox 9.0%, Gengar 6.4% |

- **Species usage** (Limitless, standings.limitlessvgc.com/0035/pokemon, T2): Kingambit 46.6%, Basculegion 44.1%, Sneasler 44.0%, Incineroar 39.7%, Floette 37.2%, Garchomp 36.2%, Charizard 30.2%, Sinistcha 28.7%, Whimsicott 21.2%, Froslass 14.5%.
- **The Charizard Y + Aerodactyl + Sylveon + Kingambit + Garchomp core** took 1st, 2nd and 7th. At Worlds (M-B), Mega Aerodactyl + Sylveon teams reached 7th (Greppi: with Charizard Y, Farigiraf, Garchomp, Kingambit) and 12th (Emilio Forbes: a Mega Raichu Y variant with Kingambit, Tsareena, Sneasler) (Limitless Worlds, T2). This is the "Aerodactyl / Kingambit / Sylveon" matchup the World Champion called "clearly unfavorable" for his team (note.com/rapid_mint7926/n/nf439490f62a6, T3).
- **The Froslass + Scovillain archetype** already converted well here: Mega Froslass rose from 14.5% of Phase 1 teams to 25.0% of Phase 2 (Victory Road, T2), and Dion took it to top 8. Weed and Leite reached the Worlds top 4 with it (see `worlds-2026.md`).

## What happened next
- M-B (June 17) added Mega Staraptor, Mega Swampert, Mega Raichu X/Y, Grimmsnarl, Gholdengo and more (Victory Road regulations page, T2).
- The Charizard Y cores stayed central at Worlds (Charizard on 45.3% of Worlds teams, Limitless T2).
- Rios and Pio Pero both reached the Frankfurt M-C top cut three months later, Rios as champion (1st) and Pio Pero 13th (Limitless and RK9).
