### Winning team: Joseph Ugarte (open team list, Limitless T2)
- **Excadrill** @ Focus Sash · Sand Rush · Jolly · Iron Head / High Horsepower / Rock Slide / Protect
- **Salamence** @ Salamencite · Intimidate · Timid · Hyper Voice / Draco Meteor / Flamethrower / Protect
- **Tyranitar** @ Tyranitarite · Sand Stream · Jolly · Rock Slide / Knock Off / Low Kick / Protect
- **Indeedee** (M) @ Choice Scarf · Psychic Surge · Modest · Expanding Force / Mystical Fire / Trick / Protect
- **Corviknight** @ Psychic Seed · Mirror Armor · Careful · Brave Bird / Power Trip / Bulk Up / Roost
- **Sneasler** @ White Herb · Unburden · Adamant · Dire Claw / Close Combat / Coaching / Protect

**MY READ:** a two-Mega team with two modes.
- **Sand:** Mega Tyranitar sets sand and Excadrill gets Sand Rush.
- **Psychic Terrain:** Indeedee-M sets terrain, which boosts Expanding Force and triggers Corviknight's Psychic Seed.
- Mega Salamence is the flexible special attacker.
- Coaching and Bulk Up / Power Trip give a late-game setup path.
- A 2026-09-28 search-engine summary says Ugarte used Coaching on Mega Tyranitar in the final (T4 lead, **unverified**).
- Ugarte ran an earlier version of the same shell (Excadrill / Tyranitar / Corviknight / Indeedee / Sneasler + Annihilape) to 8th at the Las Vegas Regional in November 2025, when the format was Scarlet/Violet (Limitless profile, limitlessvgc.com/players/1088, T2).

## Standout tech (open team lists, T2)

- **Psychic Seed users:** Corviknight (Ugarte) and Sneasler (Thompson, Wilkerson, Glick).
