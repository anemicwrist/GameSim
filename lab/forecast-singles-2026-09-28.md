## 1. How fast this field adapts (evidence from M-B → M-C)

**Data** (brief §2–3; S1/S2 = in-game snapshots 2026-09-27 vs 2026-09-06; S4 = Scout's Showdown sample):
- **New items were adopted in under three weeks.** Rocky Helmet is on 62.8% of Corviknight and 54.2% of Gyarados (neither
  had it in its M-5 top 5). Air Balloon is on 72.9% of Gholdengo, Terrain Extender on 60.8% of Indeedee (S1/S2).
- **Whole sets flipped inside one season.** Lucario went from physical Mega to special Nasty Plot Mega Z (Nasty Plot
  76.8%). Garchomp's Focus Sash fell 41.5% → 16.0% and Earthquake 99.2% → 67.3%. Gyaradosite fell 74.1% → 22.2% (S1/S2).
- **New Megas took half the Mega Evolutions** in the Showdown sample (178 of 356) (S4).
- **Species moved 10 to 30 places in one season:** Lucario 29 → 7, Gholdengo 24 → 9, Sneasler 48 → 15, Mega Delphox
  8 → 40 (S1/S2).

**My read:**
- Items and sets adapt in 1 to 3 weeks. In-game species ranks move about 5 to 15 places over 2 to 4 weeks.
- The field spent the first ~3 weeks of M-6 adopting the new tools (new Megas, Helmet, Balloon, terrain). The next 2 to
  4 weeks (this window) should bring the **answers** to those tools: anti-Veil tech, speed control above 223, and
  Salamence punishers.
- The Showdown ladder should move faster than in-game once Smogon's September M-C stats and the M-C Viability Rankings
  come out (brief §1 D and E, §8.7), because those make the win rates public.

## 4. Second-order effects for weeks 3–4 (my read)

- **If R1, R3 and R4 hit** (Veil, Gyarados and Archaludon up): Electric, Ground and Steel attacks become the most
  valuable coverage. Electric handles Gyarados and rain. Ground handles Archaludon and Lucario Z. Steel handles Alolan
  Ninetales (×4 weak). Setup sweepers (Nasty Plot Lucario Z, Swords Dance Baxcalibur) face more Taunt and Encore.
- **If R2 hits** (Metagross back): Fire, Ground, Dark and Ghost attackers (Charizard Y, Cinderace, Garchomp, Gholdengo)
  regain value against it, which could slow F5.
- **The M-7 reset** puts players of every level in the low ranks for the first days. My read: a team with few bad
  matchups climbs faster through that mix than a high-risk team does.

## 5. Counter-strategies to prepare now

**C1. Carry a Veil plan on every team (answers R1, R2).**
- Lead with, or keep in the back, a fast Steel attacker that deletes Alolan Ninetales **through** Veil.
  - Mega Lucario Z's Flash Cannon OHKOs the most common Ninetales spread (2/0/0/32/0/32, 23.4% in-game, S1) even through
    Veil: **109–129%**. Against a 32-HP Ninetales it's 91–108% (50% to OHKO). Calc-verified (method in §8).
  - Archaludon's Flash Cannon through Veil: 97–116% (87.5% to OHKO).
- Keep one anti-setup tool for whatever sets up behind the Veil: Encore (Primarina) or a phazing move.
- If the Veil problem persists in testing, add a screen-breaker. That's hypothesis
  `lab/hypotheses/metagross-veil-breaker-slot.md`.

**C2. Own the tier above 223 with a non-contact hit, and keep Salamence punishers (answers R3–R5, F1).**
- Choice Scarf Jolly Garchomp reaches 253 Speed. Its Earthquake is non-contact, so Aura Guard doesn't apply, and it
  OHKOs Mega Lucario Z: **155–184%** (calc-verified). Behind Veil it does only 77–92%, which is why C1 matters.
- Pair it with Intimidate + Rocky Helmet Gyarados. Gyarados takes Mega Salamence's unboosted Double-Edge for 61–72%, and
  then its Avalanche (doubled) OHKOs back: 105–124%.
- Add Archaludon, which resists Double-Edge (a +1 Double-Edge does 44–52%) and OHKOs back with Draco Meteor: 151–179%.
- All of this is in `lab/hypotheses/z-guard-balance.md`.

## 6. What would make this forecast wrong

- **M-7 isn't on the M-C roster, or a patch changes the tools.** New Megas, new items, or a nerf to Aura Guard, Veil or
  Rocky Helmet reset every call. The Scout confirms M-7 details.
- **Small samples.** Gyarados's 69% (n=29) and rain's 62.9% (n=35) may simply regress toward 50%. If they do, R3 and R6
  miss for sample reasons, not because the reasoning was wrong.
- **In-game "Selection Support"** (v1.2.0) shows each player a matchup indicator built from past ranked data (mechanics
  §11). My read: it may make in-game picks follow usage more closely than Showdown picks do, which slows in-game
  adaptation. UNVERIFIED.
- **Battle Pass effects.** If M-7's pass hands out a different Mega or Pokémon, in-game ranks move for reasons unrelated
  to strength.
