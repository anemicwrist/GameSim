# Sending Games to the Coach

## 1. Record the game (Android)
1. Swipe down twice to open Quick Settings and tap **Screen record** (Samsung: **Screen recorder**). If it's missing,
   tap the edit (pencil) icon and drag the tile in.
2. Set audio to **Microphone**, or **Device audio and microphone** if offered (Samsung: **Media sounds and mic**).
3. Turn on **Show touches** if your recorder offers it. It lets the Coach tell misclicks apart from decisions.
4. Start recording before Team Preview and stop after the result screen.
5. Talk while you play. Say what you think the opponent will do and why you're clicking what you click, for example
   "they'll switch to Corviknight, so I'm clicking Thunderbolt." Short is fine.

Before you queue: turn on Do Not Disturb, use Wi-Fi, and keep your phone charged.

## 2. Fill in the game log (2 minutes, right after the game)

```
MODE:                Singles / Doubles
FORMAT / SEASON:     e.g. Champions Ranked Singles, Season M-<n>
PLATFORM:            Mobile (Android)
RESULT:              W / L
MY TEAM (6):         <paste or list>
OPP TEAM (6):        <as shown at Team Preview>
MY PICKS / LEAD:     Singles: 3 picked + lead · Doubles: 4 brought + 2 leads
OPP PICKS / LEAD:    <as revealed>
RECORDING:           <link>  (mic narration: Y / N)
MY THOUGHTS:         <what I planned, where I felt it slipped>
QUESTIONS FOR COACH: <optional>
```

## 3. Send it
Upload the recording to a Google Drive folder (for example "Champions Games") and share the link in the session, or
attach it directly if your app allows. Video files stay out of this repo. The Coach keeps the review, your log, and the
link in `trainer/games/singles/`.
