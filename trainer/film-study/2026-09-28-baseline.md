# Film Study: Baseline Set (8 positions)

_Coach, 2026-09-28. Mode: Singles._

This first set does two jobs: it teaches, and it gives me a baseline read on your decisions before I see your games.

**How to answer**
- Go in order (easiest first). For each position, read the board and decide your click **before** looking at anything else.
- Send your answers in one message, one line each: `1: <your click> because <one reason>`. Guesses are fine; say when you're unsure.
- Only then open the answers: `trainer/film-study/2026-09-28-baseline-answers.md`.

**About these positions:** they come from high-rated Pokémon Showdown ladder games in the Champions Singles format (Reg M-C). That's non-official play, not in-game ranked, and the players aren't pros, so the answers show what the player did and whether it was the best play. Each board shows only what the deciding player could see at that moment: their own full team, plus whatever the opponent had revealed so far.
